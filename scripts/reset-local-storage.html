Reset Local Plant Stocks to 1000

This small utility updates the plants entry in browser localStorage so every plant's stock becomes 1000.

Direct browser console snippet

let plants=JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('plants')||'[]'); if(!plants||!plants.length) console.warn('no plants in localStorage — open site first'); plants.forEach(p=>{ if(p&&typeof p==='object') p.stock=1000 }); localStorage.setItem('plants', JSON.stringify(plants)); location.reload();

Usage: open this file in the same origin as your site (or open your site, then open the devtools console and paste the snippet above).
